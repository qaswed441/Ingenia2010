Who are we?Studio presentation

Ingenia 2010 is an industrial engineering studio with extensive experience in the development and legalization of technical installations.

At the Málaga branch we offer a close and agile service where the client deals directly with the engineer responsible for the project, ensuring fast decision-making and full control at every stage.

We have experience in electrical and energy installations and especially in photovoltaic energy projects, providing technical solutions tailored to each client and focused on efficiency and real feasibility.

We work with both companies and private clients, offering everything from one-off projects to ongoing technical collaboration.
